The 2026 Honda Ridgeline Sport is the pickup that thinks differently. Built on a unibody platform shared with the Passport and Pilot, it delivers a car-like ride quality unmatched in the mid-size truck segment — while offering a 5-foot bed with an innovative dual-action tailgate and a lockable in-bed trunk. The 3.5L V6 produces 280 hp through a 9-speed automatic with AWD standard on all trims. EPA-rated 18/24 mpg city/highway. Sport adds 18" alloy wheels and a sport appearance. Tow up to 5,000 lbs.
