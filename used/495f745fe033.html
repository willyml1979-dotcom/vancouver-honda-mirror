The 2025 Audi Q3 Progressiv 45 TFSI quattro combines performance, comfort, technology, and practicality in a premium compact SUV. It is powered by a 2.0 litre turbocharged four cylinder TFSI engine producing 228 horsepower and 258 lb ft of torque, paired with an eight speed Tiptronic automatic transmission and quattro all wheel drive. Standard exterior features include 19 inch alloy wheels, full LED lighting, a panoramic power sunroof, heated power mirrors, rain sensing wipers, and a power tailgate. Inside, the Q3 features leather seating surfaces, heated power front seats, driver seat memory, a heated leather wrapped multifunction steering wheel, dual zone automatic climate control, and Audi advanced key with push button start. Technology includes an 8.8 inch MMI touchscreen, Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth, SiriusXM, and a 10 speaker Audi sound system. Safety features include blind spot monitoring, lane departure warning, Audi pre sense systems, parking sensors, and a rear view camera.
