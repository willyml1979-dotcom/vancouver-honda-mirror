4.7L BITURBO V8 | 4MATIC ALL-WHEEL DRIVE | PREMIUM PACKAGE | DRIVER ASSISTANCE TECHNOLOGY | LUXURIOUS 7-PASSENGER SUV

Experience full-size luxury and confident performance with this Mercedes-Benz GL450 4MATIC. Powered by a 4.7L twin-turbocharged V8 engine producing 362 horsepower and 406 lb-ft of torque, it delivers impressive acceleration, smooth power delivery and confident all-wheel-drive capability.

The elegant exterior is complemented by metallic paint and aluminum running boards, giving this luxury SUV a commanding road presence. Inside, enjoy a premium leather-appointed cabin featuring Burl Walnut wood trim and a sophisticated black fabric roof liner. With three rows of seating for up to seven passengers, this GL450 offers generous space for family trips and everyday versatility.

Equipped with the Premium Package, this GL450 adds upscale comfort and convenience, while the Driving Assistance Package provides advanced driver-support technologies designed to help enhance confidence on the road. The Premium Rear Seating Package further elevates passenger comfort, making longer journeys more enjoyable.

Combining powerful V8 performance, 4MATIC all-wheel drive, premium interior finishes and versatile three-row seating, this Mercedes-Benz GL450 is an excellent choice for drivers seeking a capable and luxurious full-size SUV.
