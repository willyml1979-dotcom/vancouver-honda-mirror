2024 Toyota GR Corolla Circuit Edition — Rally-Bred Performance With Everyday Practicality

The 2024 Toyota GR Corolla Circuit Edition brings genuine motorsport engineering to the streets in one of Toyota's most exciting performance vehicles. Developed by TOYOTA GAZOO Racing and hand-built at Toyota's GR Factory in Japan, this limited-production hot hatch features a 1.6L turbocharged three-cylinder engine producing an impressive 300 horsepower and 273 lb-ft of torque. Paired with a six-speed intelligent manual transmission and Toyota's advanced GR-FOUR all-wheel-drive system, the GR Corolla delivers exhilarating acceleration, precise handling and an engaging connection between driver and machine.

Designed for driving enthusiasts who appreciate performance, engineering and exclusivity, the Circuit Edition takes the already impressive GR Corolla to another level. Its forged carbon-fibre roof, lightweight forged BBS wheels, aggressive vented hood, prominent rear spoiler and performance-focused interior create a distinctive presence on the road. Inside, the Circuit Edition features performance-inspired GR sport seats with exclusive blue contrast stitching, complemented by a leather-wrapped manual shift knob featuring a distinctive blue accent stripe. These details reinforce the vehicle's motorsport-inspired character and limited-production appeal.

Whether enjoying winding mountain roads, spirited weekend drives or everyday commuting, the GR Corolla offers the excitement of a purpose-built performance machine with the versatility of a five-door hatchback.
