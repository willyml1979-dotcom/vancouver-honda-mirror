More About This Model

The 2023 Honda Odyssey Touring makes busy family life easier with an exceptional combination of comfort, versatility and premium technology. With its spacious three-row cabin, flexible seating configurations and thoughtful family-friendly features, the Odyssey is designed to make school runs, daily errands and long road trips more enjoyable.

Powered by a 3.5L V6 engine producing 280 horsepower and 262 lb-ft of torque, paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission and front-wheel drive, the Odyssey delivers refined performance, responsive acceleration and a comfortable driving experience.
