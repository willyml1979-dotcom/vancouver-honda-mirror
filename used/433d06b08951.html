2022 Honda Civic Hatchback Sport Touring 6MT — Engaging Manual Performance Meets Premium Everyday Comfort

The 2022 Honda Civic Hatchback Sport Touring delivers an exciting combination of engaging driving dynamics, modern technology and everyday versatility. Its 1.5L turbocharged 4-cylinder engine produces 180 horsepower and 177 lb-ft of torque, paired with a 6-speed manual transmission and front-wheel drive for an engaging and connected driving experience. With its sporty hatchback design, responsive handling and practical cargo space, this Civic is an excellent choice for drivers who enjoy being connected to the road without sacrificing everyday convenience.

This Civic Sport Touring features a premium interior with leather-trimmed seating, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel and a power sunroof. Enjoy advanced connectivity through Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, along with integrated navigation and Bluetooth audio streaming. Honda Sensing safety technology adds confidence with features including Adaptive Cruise Control, Collision Mitigation Braking and Lane Keeping Assist. With its versatile hatchback configuration and premium equipment, this Civic is ready for daily commutes and weekend adventures.
