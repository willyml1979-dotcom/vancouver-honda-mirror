Powered by a responsive 1.6-litre turbocharged hybrid powertrain delivering a combined 231 horsepower and 271 lb-ft of torque, this 7-passenger SUV couples a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission with standard HTRAC™ All-Wheel Drive and Multi-Terrain Control Modes to navigate any Canadian climate with confidence. Grounded in verified Hyundai Canada specifications, the Preferred Hybrid trim offers outstanding space and practicality with one-touch power-folding second-row seats, fold-flat third-row seating, a smart power liftgate, and an innovative hidden C-pillar assist handle for easy roof-rack access.
