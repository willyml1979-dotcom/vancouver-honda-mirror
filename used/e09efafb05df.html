At the heart of this brute is Land Rover’s legendary 5.0L Supercharged V8 engine, an absolute powerhouse generating 510 horsepower and a massive 461 lb-ft of torque. Channeled through a lightning-fast 8-speed CommandShift® automatic transmission, it rockets this luxury titan from 0–100 km/h in just 5.3 seconds.

The aesthetic identity of this V8 is heavily reshaped by the optionalStealth Package.This comprehensive appearance suite strips away all standard bright chrome and silver trim, replacing them with a gorgeous satin black finish across the front grille, fog lamp surrounds, hood and fender vents, mirror caps, and tailgate script.

Between the front occupants sits aFront Centre Console Cooler Compartment, a true refrigerator unit that chills beverages on the go at the touch of a button.The sensory experience is completed by the premium Meridian™ Surround Sound System, a 19-speaker, 825-watt audiophile setup that uses advanced Trifield™ technology to deliver crystal-clear, concert-like acoustics throughout the cabin. Rounded out by a sliding panoramic roof and multi-zone climate control, this Range Rover Sport stands as the ultimate expression of luxury and performance.
